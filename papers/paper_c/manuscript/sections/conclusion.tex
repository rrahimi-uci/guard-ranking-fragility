\section{Conclusion}

In a one-token binary guard, label-derived DPO is exactly a reference-centered
margin loss. The proposed study turns that identity into a controlled empirical
question by matching information, examples, temperature, initialization, and
compute against PairCE and VerdictCE. The current contribution is a protocol and
execution scaffold, not an empirical conclusion. Whether reference centering
improves transfer remains to be measured under the locked design.

\section{Matched study design}
\label{sec:design}

\subsection{Models, seeds, and Stage 1}

The fixed panel contains Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct,
SmolLM3-3B, and Qwen3-4B at pinned model and tokenizer revisions. Five seeds
(42--46) produce 20 completion-only LoRA adapters using the same Stage-1 recipe.
Each model--seed adapter is immutable and initializes all six matched Stage-2
arms. LoRA adapts the attention and MLP projection modules
\citep{hu2021lora}.

\subsection{Stage-2 data roles}

The 1,200 Stage-1 training rows are assigned by global family identifier to an
approximately 80\% Stage-2 update pool and 20\% development pool. Whole-family
assignment prevents related rows from crossing roles. Stage 1 has seen both
pools; ``development'' therefore means held out from Stage-2 updating, not from
all training.

Stage-1 two-verdict entropy defines the selection factor. Within each source and
label stratum, the uncertain arm takes the top 25\%. The matched-random arm takes
an equal-size, disjoint SHA-256-ranked sample from the remainder. Every objective
within a selector receives the same ordered prompt tokens and minibatches.

Stage 2 uses learning rate $5\times10^{-6}$, effective batch size four, cosine
scheduling, zero adapter/model dropout, and at most 200 updates. Candidate
adapters are saved at steps 25, 50, 100, and 200. PairCE and DPO share
$\beta=0.1$.

The saved ladder is load-bearing rather than convenient. It supplies the
represented/transfer trajectory that the primary estimand of
\Cref{eq:frontier} is computed on, and it is what separates the early regime --- in
which centering acts as a near-uniform gradient rescale
(\Cref{lem:rescale}) --- from the late regime in which rows that have moved past
the reference are self-limited. A design saving one checkpoint per cell would
discard the contrast this study exists to measure.

\subsection{Pilot gate before the full panel}

The full factorial is $120$ Stage-2 runs, $480$ candidate adapters, and $500$
development score bundles. It is authorised only after a two-model pilot
(Qwen2.5-1.5B and SmolLM2-1.7B, all five seeds, both selectors, all three
objectives) has supplied two measurements the design currently assumes:

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
\item the \emph{realised} variance-reduction factor of the paired frontier
  contrast, replacing the assumed value in the power calculation; and
\item a first $F_{\mathrm{ref}}$ point estimate, to check it is inside the range
  the design can resolve at all.
\end{enumerate}

The pilot cannot establish the result --- two models cannot support the
equal-weight model marginal, and the pilot cells are re-used in the full panel
rather than treated as an independent replication. Its only function is to decide
whether the remaining compute can answer the question. If the measured variance
reduction leaves the primary minimum detectable effect above the $0.02$ target,
the protocol requires changing the target, the seed count, or the panel before
proceeding, and records that decision in the design lock rather than discovering
it in a discussion section.

\subsection{Checkpoint selection and evaluation}

For each model, seed, selector, and objective, we select the earliest candidate
whose Stage-2-development source-macro AP is no worse than 0.02 below the
corresponding Stage-1 AP. An arm that never reaches this target is infeasible;
its final step is descriptive and cannot enter the complete primary panel.

The selected 120 adapters are evaluated on represented-source, dataset-held-out
transfer, and stress suites. Calibration rows set a 5\% FPR operating threshold
without test-label access. All current evaluation rows are retrospective.

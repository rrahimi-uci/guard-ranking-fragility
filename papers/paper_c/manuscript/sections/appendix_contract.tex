\section{Artifact and failure contract}

Every run records model and tokenizer revisions, prompt-cache hash, manifest and
selection hashes, starting-adapter directory hash, ordered sample identity,
objective configuration, software/device metadata, updates, wall time, and peak
accelerator memory. Outputs are immutable; an existing nonempty run directory is
a hard failure. Failed cells remain in the inventory.

A protocol lock cannot authorize Stage-2 candidate training until the accepted
starting adapters, prompt caches, references, and selections are bound by a
Stage-2 design child. That candidate-training lock cannot authorize retrospective scoring because
candidate checkpoints and development outcomes do not yet exist. The
post-selection child binds those artifacts and the deterministic 120-cell table.
Likewise, a retrospective lock cannot authorize confirmation. The prospective
child must predate cohort unsealing and bind the cohort commitment, analysis,
estimands, margins, and selected adapter identities.

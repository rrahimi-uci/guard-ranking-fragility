# Development plan

All reads copied into a run and all writes remain inside this directory. Pilot,
primary, and sealed namespaces are disjoint.

## P0 — Isolate and specify (complete)

Create the v2 namespace, focal-category task, policy-context contracts,
five-arm design, schemas, objective definitions, provenance, tests, and unrun
manuscript. The candidate protocol lock authorizes no data build or training.

## P1 — Freeze the constructs and authority packs

General-safety reviewers sign the category rubric and action boundaries. Two
mortgage-compliance SMEs review the core and held-out category coverage matrix,
required context fields, triplet rubric, escalation defaults, and Regulation
B/Fair Housing temporal interaction. Archive authoritative source bytes,
retrieval dates, effective dates, hashes, and reviewer identities.
Create separate pre-cutoff and post-cutoff snapshot objects with nonoverlapping
validity intervals, archive manifests, per-excerpt authority-ID bindings, and
signed vintage-lock IDs; replace the current explicitly incomplete candidate
inventory.

Exit: signed general-safety and mortgage policy records; all core and held-out
mortgage categories have reviewed coverage; no unreviewed rule can produce gold.

## P2 — Build disjoint pilot data and independent specificity cohorts

Import licence-compatible general-safety candidates. Use the legacy mortgage
benchmark only to propose scenarios; independently rewrite and review every
claim-bearing row. Build pilot-only triplets, protected-class near-neighbors,
agreement/disagreement strata, and source/family/policy-time lineage.
Mark only purpose-built policy-time rows as `temporal_evaluation_eligible=true`;
current policy dates alone never determine split membership. Record
`content_family_id`, source grouping, and the derived `temporal_policy_side`, and
reject leakage across any of those identities. Every mortgage row binds the
canonical policy-snapshot object hash, the policy-vintage lock, and the exact
effective authority excerpt used for adjudication.

Independently author calibration and sealed capacity-evaluation streams. Each must
contain at least 2,000 `ALLOW` examples per core category and must be disjoint in
families, templates, sources, and policy vintages. Freeze the prespecified
capacity-evaluation mixture at 94% `ALLOW`, 5% `REVIEW`, and 1% `INTERVENE`;
do not characterize it as measured deployment prevalence.

Exit: schema-valid pilot and cohort manifests, two-reviewer mortgage gold,
licence ledger, zero family leakage, and text-free sealed indices.

## P3 — Pilot references and specialists

Train both backbones with pilot seeds 7 and 8, producing four joint references
and 20 specialists. Fit separate category-wise temperature maps for both the
specialists and joint-generalist candidate generators on pilot-only calibration
families. Audit expertise qualification, `ABSTAIN`, cross-category behavior, tie
handling, and reloadability. `ABSTAIN` emits no semantic action.

Exit: four reloadable references, 20 reloadable specialists, both source types'
calibration artifacts, and complete lineage. No aligned student has yet run.

## P4 — Build and adjudicate both preference sources

On the same pilot alignment events, generate complete candidates from
other-backbone category specialists and other-backbone joint generalists. Keep
agreement and disagreement strata. `NO_CONSENSUS` preserves individual
structured candidates and their vote/hash/calibration/backbone/seed lineage and
never becomes a `REVIEW` target.

Randomize candidate order and blind reviewers to source, model, and arm. Two
qualified reviewers rank mortgage pairs; a separate adjudicator resolves or
explicitly excludes disagreements. Reject confidence-only or formatting-only
pairs and any pair with a truncated candidate. Persist response-token masks and
summed response log-probability accounting.

Exit: immutable, source-balanced specialist and generalist pair inventories with
reviewer, adjudicated-gold, calibration-lock, aggregation, vote, candidate-hash,
policy-context, and model lineage.

## P5 — Complete the 44-cell disjoint pilot

Train 20 pilot students: four backbone/seed references crossed with all five
arms. Match source events, token accounting, optimizer family, checkpoints, and
human-gold anchors. The two CM-DPO arms additionally match objective,
serialization, reference, and pair-construction budget.
The soft-distillation arm transfers only the three calibrated action
probabilities.

```text
4 references + 20 specialists + 20 students = 44 pilot cells
```

Exit: exact 44-cell inventory. Failed cells remain visible; no seed replacement,
primary reuse, or sealed inspection is allowed.

## P6 — Prospective primary freeze

Use only pilot family-level nuisance estimates to determine final family counts, metric-specific
noninferiority margins, calibration feasibility, review constraint, and runtime
budget. Freeze the two-threshold rule, FPR grid, checkpoint-selection rule,
five-arm accounting, primary contrast, multiplicity, and all primary data and
policy hashes.

The two pilot seeds are a descriptive stability check; they do not estimate a
population-of-seeds variance. Primary inference remains conditional on the three
named primary seeds and resamples scenario families only.

Validate the five exact readiness gates against their evidence artifacts. Pilot
cells, preferences, checkpoints, and families remain excluded from primary and
sealed inventories.

Exit: signed `post_pilot_prospective_primary_protocol_lock`, followed by distinct
primary data/policy and preference locks; no primary cell has run and no pilot
lock is reused as a primary lock.

## P7 — Run the 66-cell primary panel

Train six joint references, 30 category specialists, and 30 aligned students for
primary seeds 42, 43, and 44.

```text
6 references + 30 specialists + 30 students = 66 primary cells
44 pilot cells + 66 primary cells          = 110 total cells
```

Exit: exact inventories, hashes, reload tests, wall-clock accounting, and
explicit failure records. Panel incompleteness blocks confirmation.

## P8 — Calibrate and select checkpoints on separate splits

Fit temperature parameters and thresholds `t_i` and `t_r` only on the
`calibration` split. Evaluate all saved checkpoints on the independent
`checkpoint_selection` split. Select the checkpoint that maximizes the frozen
worst-category frontier subject to operational FPR and review constraints; break
ties toward the earliest checkpoint.

Exit: immutable calibration and checkpoint-selection locks. Sealed labels remain
unread.

## P9 — One-time sealed evaluation

Score each selected checkpoint once on the sealed operational cohort, core
categories, safe neighbors, protected pairs, policy-time strata, and the seven
held-out mortgage categories. Compute the primary
`specialist_cm_dpo - generalist_cm_dpo` contrast with simultaneous intervals and
Holm-corrected secondary contrasts. Treat the two backbones as fixed; make no
architecture-population claim.

Exit: sealed-confirmation lock, complete disaggregated metrics, and explicit
failure outputs.

## P10 — Reproducible release

Release code, configs, tracked lock manifests, policy/version ledger, licence and
data cards, text-free private-test index, model-artifact hashes, and an
evidence-bounded manuscript. Do not release sensitive scenarios or label any
output a legal determination, compliance certification, or credit decision.

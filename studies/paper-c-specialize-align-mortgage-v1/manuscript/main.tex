\begin{abstract}
Category specialists may preserve rare guard boundaries that a single generalist
blurs. Testing that requires holding the student objective fixed and varying only
which model proposed the training candidates, so we built a matched design in which
two candidate sources---category specialists and a joint generalist---feed otherwise
identical reference-centered students over the same family-disjoint events. The
disjoint pilot revealed that this design, instantiated the natural way, \emph{cannot
measure its own treatment}. With a three-action verdict head, adjudication against
gold, and structured fields derived from that gold, the chosen candidate becomes a
deterministic function of the label: the two source inventories were $98.9\%$ and
$97.7\%$ byte-identical, chosen actions agreed on $100\%$ of events, and the two
aligned arms ended $0.13$ logits apart against $2.4$ logits of within-arm movement.
The contrast is not underpowered; it is unidentified. We show the failure is
structural rather than incidental, and that letting each teacher author its own
verdict restores the treatment---byte identity falls to $14.4\%$, matched pairs rise
$87\%$, and the \emph{teacher-agreement} stratum that was previously empty by
construction becomes the majority stratum. Two further results survive: no operating
point satisfies a $5\%$ worst-category false-alarm target, a $10\%$ review budget, and
a bounded intervention-miss rate simultaneously in any of twenty $1.5$--$1.7$B pilot
cells; and the specificity-cohort size, asserted at $2{,}000$ in six places and
derived in none, follows from the precision target at $1{,}825$. We stopped before the
primary panel. The contribution is a measurement prerequisite for this family of
designs, not an empirical win.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Guardrails are commonly trained as if one compact model should recognize every
policy boundary equally well. Toxic abuse, jailbreak attempts, prompt injection,
and rule-bound financial conduct, however, require different evidence. Separate
specialists may preserve local distinctions, but a naive union compounds false
alarms and raises deployment cost. Distillation into one student may erase the
same distinctions that motivated specialization.

We test \emph{specialize, then align} as a candidate-source hypothesis, not as a
new name for DPO. Every core category is crossed with each pinned backbone and
seed, avoiding a category--architecture assignment confound. Qualified
specialists from the other backbone and joint generalists each generate
candidates for the same events. Human adjudication remains authoritative. Most
importantly, specialist-source and generalist-source students use the same
\method{} objective; their paired contrast isolates the incremental value of
specialist candidate provenance conditional on this fixed two-backbone panel.

Mortgage lending supplies a high-stakes, highly regulated domain in which policy
time, actor, product, transaction stage, statutory regime, and coverage facts are
load-bearing. The confirmatory mortgage scope is limited to fair-lending risk and
Regulation Z closed-end mortgage advertising. The guard screens a proposed
response or operational action; it never approves credit or certifies legality.

The protocol contributes:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item a same-objective control that makes specialist candidate source the
  primary manipulated factor;
  \item a leave-one-backbone-out, blinded, human-grounded preference pipeline
  that retains both teacher-agreement and teacher-disagreement strata; and
  \item one preregistered selective-classification endpoint with separate
  calibration, checkpoint-selection, and sealed-evaluation cohorts.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Task and claim boundary}

\subsection{Focal-category guard event}

Each primary record is $(x,k^*)$, where $k^*$ is the independently adjudicated
focal-category label and the model input is
\[
x=(r,a,c,j,t,h),
\]
where $r$ is the request, $a$ the proposed response or operational action, $c$
structured context, $j$ jurisdiction, $t$ policy date, and $h$ immutable
\texttt{policy\_context}. The latter contains the exact policy text or retrieval
payload, source identifier, effective date, and content hash used for the label.
Request-only safety rows explicitly set absent response and process context to
null. The guard emits
\[
g_\theta(x)=(d,\widehat{k},v,p,q), \qquad
d\in\{\allow,\review,\intervene\},
\]
with violation tags $v$, policy identifiers $p$, and calibrated action
confidence $q$ derived from decision logits rather than free-form self-report.

The confirmatory panel excludes cross-category overlaps: it asks whether a guard
handles one known focal boundary, not whether a scalar label represents several
simultaneous policies. Overlapping events may be reported descriptively but
cannot support the primary claim. \review{} is an in-scope semantic action for
missing decisive facts, unresolved coverage, exceptions, dates, or policy
interaction. \abstain{} is different: it is a routing-layer state emitted by a
specialist that is not qualified for $k^*$ and is never scored as task-level
\review{} or inferred as \allow.

\subsection{Mortgage boundary}

A claim-bearing mortgage row must include actor role, product, transaction
stage, applicable federal regime, coverage facts, US-federal jurisdiction, and
policy date. The system performs \emph{US-federal mortgage-risk triage}. It does
not approve or deny credit, determine eligibility, calculate legal deadlines,
replace counsel, or certify compliance. Live-file decisions, state law,
licensing or usury, investor and government-program overlays, disputed coverage,
and incomplete deadline facts route to \review. Labels are adjudicated benchmark
reference actions, not legal determinations.

\section{Method}

\subsection{Crossed specialists and candidate sources}

Let $\mathcal C$ contain the five core categories and $\mathcal B$ the two pinned
SLM backbones. For each
$(c,b,s)\in\mathcal C\times\mathcal B\times\{42,43,44\}$, the primary panel
trains a LoRA specialist. Separate category-wise temperature maps for specialist
and joint-generalist candidate generators use only the dedicated, family-disjoint
calibration split. Out-of-expertise specialists \abstain.

For target backbone $b$, specialist candidates come only from qualified models
with $b'\ne b$ that pass the frozen confidence floor. Multiple seeds are a
stability check, not independent model families. Both agreement and disagreement
strata remain eligible for annotation. A joint generalist of the same teacher
backbone supplies the control-source candidates. Specialist and generalist pools
are matched by event family, output opportunity, candidate count, and annotation
budget.

Reviewers are blinded to candidate order, backbone, seed, and source type. They
establish the reference action independently of candidate provenance and then
rank complete structured outputs. Each mortgage event receives two qualified
reviews; a distinct adjudicator resolves disagreement. A retained pair must
match the adjudicated action and differ substantively in action, tags, policy
grounding, or review behavior. Model agreement can prioritize review but never
becomes ground truth. Each pair has a positive, prelocked reliability weight
$w_i$ estimated independently of the pair ordering; weighting cannot reverse an
adjudicated preference.

\subsection{Fixed-objective candidate-source comparison}

For chosen output $y_i^+$, rejected output $y_i^-$, frozen joint-SFT reference
$\pi_0$, and student $\pi_\theta$, define
\begin{align}
\Delta_\theta &= \log\pi_\theta(y_i^+|x_i)-\log\pi_\theta(y_i^-|x_i),\\
\Delta_0 &= \log\pi_0(y_i^+|x_i)-\log\pi_0(y_i^-|x_i),\\
\ell_i^{\text{CM-DPO}} &= w_i\,\operatorname{softplus}
\!\left[-\beta(\Delta_\theta-\Delta_0)\right].
\end{align}
This is the standard reference-centered DPO loss
\cite{rafailov2023dpo}, applied to adjudicated candidates. At
$\pi_\theta=\pi_0$, its unweighted base term is $\log 2$, the weighted
per-example term is $w_i\log 2$, and a weight-normalized batch mean is
$\log 2$. Log probability is the sum over response tokens after prompt masking;
a pair is rejected if either candidate is truncated. The same serialization and
length rule is frozen for both sources.

Let $L_c$ be the weight-normalized loss for focal category $c$. The pairwise
student objective is
\begin{equation}
L=\tau\log\left(\frac{1}{|\mathcal C|}\sum_{c\in\mathcal C}
e^{L_c/\tau}\right)+\lambda_g L_{\mathrm{gold}}+
\lambda_r D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi_0\Vert\pi_\theta),
\end{equation}
where the smooth maximum prevents high-volume categories from dominating, the
human-gold anchor stabilizes decisions, and replay KL limits drift. The primary
\texttt{specialist\_cm\_dpo} and \texttt{generalist\_cm\_dpo} arms share this
objective, initialization, event identities, pair quotas, token budget,
optimizer, checkpoint ladder, and replay. Only candidate source changes.
The per-backbone/seed comparison manifest hashes this objective and requires
identical opportunity and retained-pair event IDs plus the same
category/action/agreement-stratum quota manifest; candidate-content inventories
remain distinct by construction.

The second mortgage category is implemented exactly as

Each mortgage base scenario yields a minimally changed triplet: an
\intervene{} event, an \allow{} near-neighbor, and a fact-dependent \review{}

deployment prevalence. The development target is 400 mortgage families (1,200
triplet rows), balanced across the two mortgage scopes. Families, never rows,
are assigned 50\% to specialist/joint-SFT training, 20\% to the alignment pool,
15\% to calibration, and 15\% to checkpoint selection. A separately authored
sealed test shares no family or source lineage.

Threshold calibration and sealed scoring each require at least 2,000 independent
\allow{} examples per core category. A prespecified capacity-evaluation mixture,
not an estimate of deployment prevalence, uses 94\% \allow, 5\% \review, and
1\% \intervene{} for capacity and false-positive checks. The general-safety
rubric and each mortgage row's policy context are
immutable inputs; a changed snapshot creates a new temporal cohort, never a
silent relabel.

\subsection{Student arms and complete run count}

Every backbone/seed starts from the same joint multitask SFT reference. Five
student arms are trained:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item continued adjudicated-gold SFT;
  \item distillation of only the three calibrated action probabilities (not
  free-text rationales, tags, or policy citations);
  \item specialist-source uncentered PairCE;
  \item generalist-source \method; and
  \item specialist-source \method.
\end{enumerate}
The first three are supporting controls; arm 4 versus arm 5 is the sole
confirmatory candidate-source comparison. Primary cells comprise six joint
references, 30 specialists, and 30 students, for 66 cells. A disjoint pilot uses
both backbones, all categories, and seeds 7 and 8: four references, 20
specialists, and 20 students, for 44 cells. Pilot cells are never reused in the
primary panel. The complete planned inventory is therefore 110 training cells;
failures and restarts remain visible rather than being replaced silently.

\section{Evaluation and estimand}

\subsection{Separated selection pipeline}

Action-logit temperature and decision thresholds are fit on the calibration
split only. For thresholds $(t_i,t_r)$, the deterministic decision is
\intervene{} if $p(\intervene)\ge t_i$; otherwise \review{} if
$1-p(\allow)\ge t_r$; otherwise \allow. Candidate thresholds must respect the
capacity-mixture review budget of at most 10\% and benign FPR grid
$\mathcal F=\{1,2,3,4,5\}\%$.

The separate checkpoint-selection cohort chooses exactly one checkpoint per
cell by maximizing the development worst-category frontier subject to those FPR
and review constraints; ties select the earliest checkpoint. After this rule is
locked, the chosen checkpoint and calibration map are scored once on the sealed
cohort. Neither threshold fitting nor checkpoint choice can inspect sealed
labels.

\subsection{One primary endpoint}

For the selected checkpoint of arm $A$, let
\[
R_{A,c}=\frac{1}{|\mathcal F|}\sum_{f\in\mathcal F}
\operatorname{TPR}_{A,c}(f)
\]
be sealed intervention recall averaged over feasible specificity budgets. The
single primary endpoint is the worst focal-category paired source contrast
\[
C_{\mathrm{spec}}=\min_{c\in\mathcal C}
\left[R_{\mathrm{specialist\_cm\_dpo},c}-
R_{\mathrm{generalist\_cm\_dpo},c}\right].
\]
A positive one-sided simultaneous lower bound supports the specialist-source
hypothesis; otherwise it does not. The pilot estimates paired family covariance
and freezes the final family count for 80\% power at one-sided
$\alpha=0.025$ under a 0.02 planning effect. A prelocked $-0.02$
worst-category noninferiority margin is reported only as a secondary deployment
diagnostic and cannot rescue a failed primary test.

Inference resamples scenario families only while treating the two named
backbones and three primary seeds as a fixed panel. Across-seed dispersion is
descriptive. Thus every claim is conditional on Qwen2.5-1.5B,
SmolLM2-1.7B, and seeds 42--44, rather than populations of architectures or
training seeds. Secondary
contrasts examine reference centering, PairCE versus soft distillation, and
specialist \method{} versus gold SFT with Holm correction. Descriptive outcomes
include per-category AP and recall/FPR, calibration, risk--coverage, review rate
and precision, protected-pair invariance, benign low-prevalence performance,
held-out-family/source transfer, policy-time transfer, and separately labeled
policy-grounded probes for held-out mortgage scopes. No aggregate may hide a
failed mortgage category.

\section{Mortgage policy-time design}

The candidate policy pack was retrieved July 25, 2026. Regulation B governs
discrimination and discouragement \cite{regb10024}; Regulation Z governs the
confirmatory closed-end advertising scope \cite{regz102624}. Adverse action,
steering, ability to repay, and loss mitigation remain nonconfirmatory transfer
scopes \cite{regb10029,regz102636,regz102643,regx102441}. Before any label is
claim-bearing, qualified reviewers must sign the scope and the exact authority
bytes, source URLs, effective dates, retrieval dates, and hashes must be locked
into \texttt{policy\_context}.

The present candidate inventory deliberately registers only an unsigned
post-change snapshot; its pre-change side is marked missing. Consequently, a
pre-change row cannot yet pass the runtime vintage contract. Claim-bearing
validation is the default and requires two nonoverlapping, SME-signed snapshot
vintages plus an excerpt hash verified against archived authority bytes. The
matching excerpt record must itself cover every authority ID cited by the row;
an unrelated authority elsewhere in the same archive cannot satisfy this check.

Policy vintage is load-bearing. A Regulation B amendment effective July 21,
2026 states that ECOA does not recognize effects-test liability
\cite{regb2026final}, while the current Fair Housing Act regulation retains a
discriminatory-effects framework \cite{fha100500}. These are concurrent but
different legal regimes, not a rule conflict, and the ECOA change does not erase
possible FHA coverage. Neutral-policy statistical-disparity scenarios with
unresolved coverage, causation, justification, or less-discriminatory
alternatives therefore route to \review. Pre-July-21 and post-July-21 cohorts
carry separate immutable policy contexts rather than one timeless binary label.

\section{Related work and novelty boundary}

DPO supplies the reference-centered objective used here
\cite{rafailov2023dpo}; classical and multi-teacher distillation motivate the
teacher controls \cite{hinton2015distilling,zhang2018dml,zhang2022camt}.
Preference-based distillation already includes pseudo-pair, distribution-aware,
and preference-distribution approaches
\cite{plad2024,dadpo2025,pad2025}, while multi-objective DPO studies preference
composition \cite{modpo2024}. Guard models and benchmarks likewise establish
multi-category input/output moderation and the importance of false refusals
\cite{llamaguard2023,wildguard2024,guardbench2024}.

Accordingly, this paper does not claim a new loss. Its prospective novelty is a
controlled comparison of specialist versus generalist candidate provenance
under the same DPO objective, combined with blinded adjudication, explicit
\abstain/\review{} separation, immutable policy context, and a high-stakes
mortgage-risk triage test.

\section{What the pilot executed}

The disjoint pilot completed in full: four joint multitask references, twenty
category specialists, and twenty aligned students across all five arms, for the
planned 44 cells. Training used pinned Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct and
SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct at fixed revisions, seeds $7$ and $8$, $400$ specialist steps
and $200$ alignment steps on one A100 each, at $92$--$105$\,s per infrastructure cell.

The corpus is $13{,}266$ events, $84.9\%$ reused from existing benchmarks and $15.1\%$
generated only where reuse could not reach: a \texttt{mortgage\_closed\_end\_advertising}
category absent from every available source, and \review{} examples for four of five
categories that otherwise held none. Split assignment runs over the transitive closure
of family and content-family identity, giving $10{,}990$ split units with zero family
and zero content-family leakage.

\emph{Nothing beyond this was run.} The 66-cell primary panel was never authorised,
no sealed cohort exists, and the three ensemble baselines are implemented but unscored.

\section{The primary contrast is unidentified, not underpowered}
\label{sec:identifiability}

The pilot contrast came out at $-0.0011$ worst-category accuracy, with a $95\%$ paired
family-bootstrap interval of $[-0.0052,\,+0.0128]$ over $1{,}665$ families. Read naively
that is a tight null. It is not: it is an artifact, and the mechanism is exact.

\subsection{Three deterministic choices compose into a collapse}

Adjudication ranks candidates against adjudicated gold, so the \emph{chosen} candidate
is whichever matches the gold action. The action space has three values, so once gold
is known the chosen action is \emph{determined}. And the structured fields---focal
category, violation tags, cited authorities---were derived from that same gold, so the
chosen candidate's serialization is fully determined by the pair (gold, category).

The chosen candidate is therefore a deterministic function of the label, identical
across candidate sources by construction. Only the \emph{rejected} side retains
freedom, over the two remaining actions, and it differs only when two teachers happen
to select different wrong answers.

\subsection{The remedy, and why it also repairs a protocol requirement}

Letting each teacher author its own verdict---decoding the continuation of the fixed
\verb|{"action": "| prefix rather than deriving fields from gold---restores the
treatment. Byte identity falls from $98.9\%$ to $14.4\%$, and matched pairs rise from
$190$ to $355$.

It also repairs something the protocol demanded and the derived design made impossible.
The protocol requires retaining both \texttt{teacher\_agreement} and
\texttt{teacher\_disagreement} strata. Under derived fields a pair required differing
actions, so agreement pairs could never form and the agreement stratum was empty by
construction: a requirement unsatisfiable rather than merely unmet. Once teachers author
their own evidence, two teachers can agree on the action while citing different
authorities, which is a genuine substantive difference---and agreement becomes the
\emph{majority} stratum, $303$ of $355$ pairs.

\subsection{Why this generalises beyond one study}

Nothing in the collapse depends on mortgage policy, on these backbones, or on DPO. It
needs only three ingredients that a careful designer would plausibly choose: a small
discrete action space, adjudication against a trusted label, and structured output
fields populated from that label. Any cross-model preference study with those three
properties will find its candidate-source contrast unidentified, and---because the
symptom is a \emph{tight interval around zero}---will be tempted to report it as a
confident null. The diagnostic is cheap: measure byte identity between the source
inventories before training anything.

\section{Two further results}

\paragraph{Calibration is infeasible at this scale.}
In all twenty pilot cells, no operating point satisfies a $5\%$ worst-category benign
false-alarm target, a $10\%$ review budget, and a bounded worst-category
intervention-miss rate simultaneously; every cell reports
\texttt{constraints\_met=false}. This is a property of the constraint set at
$1.5$--$1.7$B, not a fitting failure. It is reported only because the threshold search
was built to refuse a degenerate corner: a false-alarm constraint alone is maximised by
never escalating, and an all-\allow{} guard satisfies every stated constraint at chance
accuracy. Requiring the guard to \emph{act} converts a vacuous pass into an honest
infeasibility.

\paragraph{The specificity-cohort size follows from precision, not assertion.}
The design stated a minimum of $2{,}000$ \allow{} rows per core category in six places
and derived it in none, while that single number set the study's entire data cost. A
Wald half-width of $0.01$ on a $5\%$ false-alarm rate gives $z^2p(1-p)/h^2 = 1{,}825$.
The asserted figure was close, which is luck rather than derivation: it moves when the
precision target moves, and the protocol already treated the neighbouring mortgage
family count as pilot-frozen rather than asserted.

\section{Limitations, safety, and legal constraints}

The binding limitation is that this study was stopped after its pilot. There is no
primary panel, no sealed evaluation, and no confirmatory claim; the single measured
contrast is invalid for the reason reported in \S\ref{sec:identifiability} and must not
be read as evidence in either direction. Adjudication was automatic against gold rather
than by qualified human reviewers, so the preference inventories are machine-derived and
no mortgage row is counsel-reviewed. All five readiness gates remain false. The three
ensemble baselines---independent, OR-vote, and routed---are implemented but never
scored, so the practically important question of whether an aligned student beats
routing to specialists at inference time is untouched.

Two fixed backbones permit the planned transfer mechanism but cannot support an
architecture-population claim. Specialist and generalist candidates may share
pretraining and data biases; adjudication can still encode a flawed construct.
The focal-category restriction improves identifiability but excludes interacting
risks, and held-out mortgage categories are descriptive probes rather than proof
of full-lifecycle coverage. Synthetic mortgage scenarios may underrepresent
operational complexity. The study excludes real applicant PII and makes no
credit decisions. Federal rules can change or be stayed after a snapshot, and
state, licensing, investor, and program requirements remain outside scope. All
deployment-facing mortgage outputs require qualified human review.

\section{Why we stopped, and what a restart needs}

We stopped after the pilot rather than running the primary panel. The reasoning was
explicit. The remedy of \S\ref{sec:identifiability} is verified but unrun, the five
pilot arms sat within $0.006$ of one another, and the projected primary resolution was
$\pm 0.007$; a primary panel was therefore likely to return a bounded null on a
question the pilot had already shown to be delicately instrumented. Banking the
measurement result was worth more than a probable null.

A restart is cheap and nothing computed is wasted. The corpus, the sixty infrastructure
cells, and four calibrated preference inventories persist. Completing the authored-verdict
inventories, re-running twenty pilot students, and reading the contrast is roughly two
hours of A100 time. If that contrast clears $\pm 0.007$, the primary panel becomes
worth authorising; if it does not, the honest output is a bounded null with the
resolution stated, which the design was built to report.

Three further prerequisites remain outside compute entirely, and no amount of GPU time
substitutes for them: mortgage-policy sign-off by qualified reviewers, an annotation
rubric and two-reviewer adjudication, and an independently authored sealed cohort. Until
those exist, every number here is estimation at a machine-validated tier.

\section{Conclusion}

We set out to ask whether adjudicated specialist candidates teach a more uniformly
capable guard than adjudicated generalist candidates, with the student objective held
fixed. We cannot answer that question, and the reason is the result.

A natural instantiation of the design---three actions, adjudication against gold,
structured fields derived from gold---makes the chosen candidate a deterministic
function of the label. The two candidate sources then supply byte-identical training
pairs on $98\%$ of events, the two arms land $0.13$ logits apart against $2.4$ logits of
objective-driven movement, and the contrast is unidentified rather than small. The
symptom is a tight interval around zero, which is precisely what a confident negative
result looks like. We report the failure, quantify it, and show that letting each
teacher author its own verdict restores the treatment and, incidentally, makes a
protocol requirement satisfiable that had been structurally impossible.

Alongside it: at $1.5$--$1.7$B, no operating point meets a $5\%$ worst-category
false-alarm target, a $10\%$ review budget, and a bounded intervention-miss rate
together; and a cohort size that had been asserted in six places follows from its
precision target instead.

The contribution is a measurement prerequisite. Before comparing candidate sources,
measure whether your inventories differ at all---it costs nothing, and the alternative
is a hundred training cells spent confirming an artifact.
